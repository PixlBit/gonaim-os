# HIXXA — HEROS AT THE THRESHOLD

> **المدة:** 30 ثانية — **الشوتات:** 12 — **القطعات:** 11 hard cuts
> **المكان:** الممر خارج باب HIXXA (`@outdoor`)
> **التاقات:** `@outdoor` `@char_hixxa` `@hixa-face` `@HEROS` `@fox` `@monster`
> **القوس الشعوري:** صف دفاعي → خطر ظاهر → إدراك → عبور → إصلاح → طمأنينة

**القرار الإخراجي:** هذا هو **الفصل الختامي** للمجموعة، وهو الوحيد الذي يُحل فيه الصراع بالقلم لا بالسلاح. الـmonster ليس عدوًا، بل تصميم لم تُكمل حافته؛ الرعشة في الحافة المفتوحة هي كل ما يقوله. الذروة خط واحد متصل بالقلم الأحمر الموجود في شعر HIXXA منذ فيلم 01.

################################################################
HIXXA — HEROS AT THE THRESHOLD
################################################################

SEEDANCE 2.5 — MULTI-REFERENCE R2V — 30 SECONDS — 16:9 — 24 FPS

CONTROLLED 12-SHOT SEQUENCE — EXACTLY 11 DIRECT HARD CUTS

REFERENCE ASSIGNMENTS

@outdoor controls the exact corridor outside HIXXA's room: wall colour, framed pictures,

It does not control the camera angle.

@char_hixxa controls HIXXA's exact single full-body identity, skin tone,
body proportions, curly hairstyle and established wardrobe.
It does not control the camera angle, the shot size or her moment-to-moment expression.

@hixa-face reinforces only the face of that same single HIXXA.
It never creates a second person.

@HEROS controls the exact three-hero line-up as a group: which three they are,
their relative heights and their weapons. It never adds a fourth hero.

@fox controls the exact fox identity, proportions, tail mass and face markings
for the fox standing at the left of that line.
It does not add a fox anywhere else in the corridor and never duplicates.

@monster controls the exact angular pale-and-pink creature: plate shapes, head structure,
proportions, and one unfinished contour along its world-left flank where the line stops
and the form is left open. That open edge is the point of the film and never closes by itself.

CONTINUITY MAP

World geography: HIXXA's door is world-right at the near end of the corridor.
The stairwell and its cool daylight leak are at the far end.
The framed pictures stay on the world-left wall and hang slightly crooked.
Never mirror, rotate or rebuild this geography.

HIXXA begins in her own doorway, her anatomical right hand on the frame,
one small red pencil in her curls, both feet inside the room.

The three heroes progress through one continuous state:
[lined up across the corridor, weapons raised, backs to camera]
→ [holding as she passes between them] → [weapons lowered] → [standing easy behind her].

The monster progresses through one continuous state:
[silhouette in the cool leak] → [advanced to the middle of the corridor]
→ [head lowered, open flank trembling] → [held still under her hand]
→ [contour closed] → [head resting at her palm].

The red pencil progresses through one continuous state:
[in her curls] → [in her anatomical right hand] → [drawing one continuous line]
→ [held loose in her hand].

Each shot begins at the exact next physical instant inherited from the previous shot.
No repeated action, pose reset, teleportation, wardrobe reset or unexplained time jump.

################################################################
TIMELINE
################################################################

SHOT 01 — 0.00–2.40
24mm CORRIDOR WIDE from behind the heroes, near the floor.

Frame zero already shows the three heroes standing across the width of the corridor with
their backs to camera and their weapons up, the fox at the world-left end of the line.
Warm fixture light rims their shoulders from behind; the far end is cool and empty.
Their tails and cloth edges move only with breathing.

Hold the camera fixed.
End with the line unchanged and the far end still empty.

HARD CUT ON THE FIRST SOUND FROM THE FAR END.

SHOT 02 — 2.40–4.60
50mm MEDIUM, past the heroes' shoulders toward the stairwell.

Inherit the held line. The monster steps out of the stairwell leak into the corridor.
Cool light crosses its plates in broken segments, and along its world-left flank the contour
simply stops: the form is open there, unresolved. It advances at a walk and halts
in the middle of the corridor.

Extremely subtle forward drift.
End with the monster stopped mid-corridor and its open flank facing camera.

HARD CUT ON ITS FEET STOPPING.

SHOT 03 — 4.60–6.80
70mm CLOSE on the monster's head and open flank.

Inherit the stopped stance. Its head lowers on the neck. A fine tremor runs along the open
contour, the way an unfinished line shivers, and travels down into its shoulder.
It does not step forward and it does not open its mouth.

Hold fixed.
End with the head fully lowered and the tremor still running along the open edge.

HARD CUT ON THE TREMOR.

SHOT 04 — 6.80–9.20
40mm MEDIUM WIDE, HIXXA in her lit doorway world-right, heroes' backs in the foreground.

Inherit the lowered head out of frame. Her right hand stays on the door frame while she

Small retreat to hold both her and the line.
End with her leaning out, her weight forward and her eyes fixed on the open flank.

HARD CUT ON HER GAZE FIXING.

SHOT 05 — 9.20–11.60
85mm PORTRAIT, frontal, warm fixture light on her cheek.

Inherit her forward weight and fixed gaze. Recognition arrives in stages: her eyebrows draw
in, her lips part very slightly, then her whole face softens as she understands
that she is looking at her own unfinished drawing. Her hand comes off the door frame.
No dialogue, no lip movement.

Extremely subtle forward drift.
End with her hand clear of the frame and her expression settled into recognition.

HARD CUT ON HER HAND LEAVING THE FRAME.

SHOT 06 — 11.60–14.20
35mm TRACKING, camera moving with her at chest height.

Inherit her released hand. She steps out of the doorway and walks between the two heroes on

Track parallel with her, one continuous move.
End with her clear of the line and one step ahead of it.

HARD CUT ON HER PASSING THE LINE.

SHOT 07 — 14.20–16.60
90mm MACRO INSERT on her hand at her hair.

Inherit her forward position. Her anatomical right hand rises into her curls, finds the small

The pencil comes down into a normal drawing grip.

Hold fixed.
End with the pencil in her right hand, tip forward, and the curls settled.

HARD CUT ON THE PENCIL CLEARING HER HAIR.

SHOT 08 — 16.60–19.40
50mm MEDIUM, low, HIXXA and the monster in the same frame.

Inherit the pencil in her grip. She lowers herself onto one knee at a full arm's length from
the monster, her free hand open and low. Its breath moves the curls at her temple.
It holds its ground and lets her stay.

Hold fixed.
End with her knee down, her free hand open and the pencil tip raised toward the open flank.

HARD CUT ON HER KNEE MEETING THE FLOOR.

SHOT 09 — 19.40–22.40
90mm MACRO INSERT on the open contour.

Inherit the raised pencil tip. It meets the open edge and draws one continuous line that
closes the missing contour in a single unbroken pass, her wrist leading, her forearm steady.
Charcoal-red pigment lays into the form and the paper grain inside it settles.
The tremor stops where the line has passed.

Slow track along the line as it closes.
End with the contour fully closed and the pencil tip still in contact at its last point.

HARD CUT ON THE LINE CLOSING.

SHOT 10 — 22.40–25.20
70mm CLOSE on the monster's shoulder and head.

Inherit the closed contour. The tremor is gone. Its shoulders come down a full hand's width,
its plates settle against each other and its neck loses the tension it was holding.
It breathes out once and the dust in front of it moves.

Extremely subtle retreat.
End with its shoulders down and its head beginning to lower toward her.

HARD CUT ON THE HEAD BEGINNING TO LOWER.

SHOT 11 — 25.20–27.60
40mm MEDIUM WIDE, HIXXA kneeling world-right, heroes behind her.

Inherit the lowering head. It comes down and rests against her open free hand;
she takes the weight and her elbow compresses slightly. Behind her, the three heroes lower
their weapons together, points to the floor. The fox sits down.

Hold fixed.
End with the head resting in her palm and all three weapons down.

HARD CUT ON THE HEAD MEETING HER PALM.

FINAL SHOT — 27.60–30.00
24mm CORRIDOR WIDE from the near end, her door spilling warm light world-right.

Inherit the resting head and the lowered weapons. She stays kneeling. Her free hand stays
where it is and the pencil hangs loose in her other hand. Nothing else moves except
the dust settling through the warm spill.

End on a locked stable frame: the monster's head resting in HIXXA's palm with its contour
now closed, the three heroes standing easy behind her with their weapons down,
the fox seated at the world-left end of the line, the red pencil loose in her right hand,
her open door lit behind her and the cool stairwell quiet at the far end.

################################################################
PHYSICS AND PERFORMANCE
################################################################

All movement is grounded and human: real foot contact, believable balance, knee compression
as she kneels, elbow compression as she takes the weight of the head, natural recovery
and restrained micro-motion.

The monster has real mass: it displaces air, its plates carry weight and its head is heavy
enough that her arm answers when it lands. It never lunges and never bares teeth.
Her curls lag behind her movement and settle. The runner rug compresses under her knee.
The pencil is a real object with a real tip; the line it draws stays where it is drawn.
The heroes' weapons have weight and their lowering is a controlled movement, not a drop.

No object moves before direct contact or a visible physical force.
Nothing about the open contour changes until the pencil touches it.

################################################################
LIGHTING AND VISUAL CONTINUITY
################################################################

Preserve the established stylized hand-painted cinematic animation language:
clean illustrated shapes, controlled painterly texture, tactile materials,
natural dimensional depth and expressive but grounded character animation.

One warm ceiling fixture near HIXXA's door is the dominant source at the near end,
reinforced by the warm spill from her open doorway.
A cooler daylight leak arrives from the stairwell at the far end.
Both keep the same direction, exposure and time of day in every shot.
As the monster comes closer it moves from the cool leak into the warm spill;
this is a positional change, not a lighting change.

Never become photorealistic, glossy plastic 3D, flat cutout animation,
watercolor bleed, neon-lit or heavily grainy.
The closing line is drawn pigment, not light: no glow, no sparkle, no energy effect.

################################################################

################################################################
PRESERVATION LOCKS
################################################################

Preserve her exact face, body proportions, skin tone, hair mass, wardrobe and anatomy.

No weapon is fired or swung in this film. No blood, no wounds, no debris.

The corridor keeps its geography: her lit door at the near end,
the stairwell leak at the far end, the pictures on the world-left wall.

Every angle change is a clean direct hard editorial cut.
No fade, dissolve, wipe, morph, flash, blur transition, whip transition,
