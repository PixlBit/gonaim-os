## مكانه في السلسلة

داخل من `P04`. خارج على: هي عرفت إن الجدران دي رسوماتها هي اللي رمتها.

## اللي اتظبط

- استُبدل بلوك الستايل بتاعهم بالكامل بـ **MASTER VISUAL STYLE — TIER 2 (OPERATIVE)** تحت بانر
  `MOOD AND RENDER CONTRACT`: العالم مجسّم بالكامل، الوجوه منحوتة بأسطح مظللة، الشعر كُتَل قابلة
  للقراءة، ظل هجين (إضاءة فراغية + أشكال ظل مرسومة)، الرسم **على** الأجسام مش فلتر فوق الصورة،
  لا كونتور موحّد، والـ CRITICAL DISTINCTION: العالم مجسّم والرسومات مسطّحة.
- أُضيف بلوك **HANDS**: تشريح اليد الصحيح + آثار الألوان الجافة على الجلد فقط، والأظافر تفضل نضيفة
  بدون لون على صفيحة الظفر.
- أُضيف بلوك **SCALE** بمرساة نسبية (الشيء + مقارنته بحاجة في الكادر)، مش أبعاد مطلقة لوحدها.
- أُضيف **AUDIO TIMELINE** بنوافذ زمنية مطابقة لنوافذ الشوتات بالظبط.
- العناوين اتحوّلت لصيغة `SHOT n — xx.xx–yy.yy` بدل `0:xx–0:yy`، والبانرات اتوحّدت.
- البرومبت ده **جيل واحد** (`8.00s`) — الـ SHOT عناوين قَطْع جوّه الكليب، مش برومبتات منفصلة.
- لحظة التعرّف هي قلب الشوت: الوجه بيشتغل — عين بتتوسّع، فك بيرتخي — مش مجرد نظرة.
- الحلقات بتلف بإيقاع زيوتروب لكن **الرسمة الواحدة ما بتتحركش جوّه نفسها**؛ اللي بيتحرك هو
  الحلقة اللي شايلة الصفحات. ده اللي بيخلي الفرق بين «فليب بوك» و«رسمة اتحيت».

################################################################
HIXXA R2V 05 — THREE ZOETROPE RINGS / REJECTED DESIGN RECOGNITION

################################################################

SEEDANCE 2.5 — MULTI-REFERENCE R2V — 8.0 SECONDS — 16:9 — 24 FPS

CONTROLLED FOUR-SHOT SEQUENCE — EXACTLY THREE DIRECT HARD CUTS

################################################################
SCENE AND MODE
################################################################

Construct the starting state from Elements and the explicit continuity below. Do not use first-frame or last-frame images. This is one continuous feet-first descent covered by four coherent angles with exactly three cuts. Three physical paper zoetrope rings rotate independently around one stationary open shaft; flat sequential drawings create the illusion of moving fox, cat and dragon studies while HIXXA falls through their open centers.

################################################################
REFERENCE ASSIGNMENTS
################################################################

Use @char_hixxa and @hixa-face for the same single HIXXA. Use @fox strictly for the damaged winged-fox design repeated as varied flat studies on the outer ring, @cat strictly for the clockwork-cat design drawn on the middle ring, and @dragon strictly for angular white-dragon studies on the inner ring. References control drawing identity only; no physical companion appears.

################################################################
HANDS
################################################################

Her hands are a working artist's hands: five fingers with one opposing thumb, correct joint
count and proportion, natural knuckle relief and tendon lines, and grips that close with real
contact against what they hold.

Dried paint is worn into the skin from long working days — soft pale blue and cyan along the
sides of the thumb and across the finger pads, a little dusty pink, cream flecks over the
knuckles. It is matte, faded and absorbed, following the skin's own creases and sitting under
the shading rather than on top of it. The fingernails stay clean bare nail: no colour on the
nail plate. The stains live on skin only.

################################################################
SCALE
################################################################

HIXXA is about 165 centimetres and her body is the scale reference.
The three rings are large physical structures whose open centres she passes through with room to
spare — each opening several times her shoulder width, never a tight squeeze. One panel on a ring
is an ordinary drawing sheet. Each crumpled ball is about 8 centimetres.

################################################################
STARTING CONTINUITY
################################################################

HIXXA falls feet-first at unchanged speed toward the open center of three concentric paper rings. Her exact red pencil remains tucked inside her curls. Both ears are bare. Exactly thirty crumpled-paper balls descend in several visible lanes around and ahead of her.

The rings are large physical structures built from connected cream panels. Their drawings are flat ink, graphite and restrained color bonded to the surfaces. Ring rotation creates all apparent animal movement; drawings never extrude or detach.

################################################################
CONTROLLED FOUR-SHOT TIMELINE
################################################################

SHOT 1 — 0.00–1.45 — OUTER FOX RING

24 mm axial plunge through the outer ring. Camera falls behind and slightly above HIXXA while preserving vertical screen direction. The outer physical ring turns clockwise around the stationary central opening.

Repeated winged-fox studies pass in sequential panels: rust-orange body tests, one complete ragged black wing and one pale unfinished construction-line wing. Apparent wing beats come only from different flat poses passing camera. Ten leading balls clear the center; the remaining twenty follow around HIXXA without striking panels.

MATCH CUT at 1.45 on a black painted wing shape crossing frame.

SHOT 2 — 1.45–3.20 — MIDDLE CLOCKWORK-CAT RING

35 mm close three-quarter inside the second ring, same side of the descent axis. Continue HIXXA's exact velocity and body phase. The middle ring turns counterclockwise more slowly than the outer ring.

Clockwork-cat studies pass behind her: teal folded body, one cyan glass-eye drawing, one cream clock-eye drawing, loose chest-dial construction and a single coil-tail study. HIXXA turns only her head toward the panels while torso and feet remain aligned with the fall. Her expression shifts from fear to brief recognition of her rejected design.

The thirty balls preserve their lanes through the stationary center.

HARD CUT at 3.20 as one cream panel edge wipes frame downward.

SHOT 3 — 3.20–5.55 — INNER DRAGON RING

28 mm wide side view through the innermost ring. The inner ring accelerates once, generating a strong but clearly flat running-and-wingbeat illusion from angular white-dragon panels. Each study contains one torn membrane and one broken construction side.

HIXXA tucks her anatomical RIGHT shoulder to avoid one protruding paper hinge. The nearest ball lanes split cleanly around the hinge and rejoin without collision or count change. The red pencil stays fixed in her curls.

MATCH CUT at 5.55 when a torn dragon outline aligns with a real tear opening beneath the rings.

SHOT 4 — 5.55–8.00 — EXIT TEAR / CAST SHADOW REVEAL

24 mm camera falls just ahead of HIXXA, looking back and slightly upward as she passes through the real tear. The ring system recedes above and remains physically intact.

The tear expands into one open cream-paper chasm. A huge asymmetric dragon-shaped shadow already stretches across the far wall: one complete wing silhouette and one broken skeletal construction side. The shadow is produced only by fixed torn paper layers between the directional light and canyon wall. It has no volume and never leaves the wall.

HIXXA looks toward the shadow while continuing downward. Exactly thirty balls follow through the tear in staggered depth lanes. No ring, panel or animal follows as a living object.

################################################################
CAMERA AND PHYSICS
################################################################

Keep the central descent stable while the rings supply rotational energy. Never rotate the camera, HIXXA and rings together as one flat image.
Each ring has one physical rotation direction and speed. No wobbling, warping, duplicated panels or changing ring count.
The drawings follow every fold, crease, perspective change and surface shadow with zero texture crawl.
Preserve one HIXXA, one red pencil, exactly thirty balls and one downward gravity direction.
No living fox, cat or dragon; no detached teeth, eyes, wings, tails or paws.

################################################################
LIGHTING AND AUDIO
################################################################

Use one motivated directional source crossing the rings to create clear alternating panel light and shadow, then cast the dragon silhouette across the chasm wall. Do not let the shadow self-illuminate.

Audio: layered paper-wheel rotation, panel clicks, close ball passes, falling wind and one low recognition motif. No animal voices, dialogue, narration, captions or subtitles.

################################################################
MOOD AND RENDER CONTRACT
################################################################
A hand-painted 2D animated film. Every frame reads as an illustration painted by hand over
drawn construction, and never as a 3D render.

Form is DRAWN, not rendered. Faces, hands and bodies carry real construction, correct
perspective and believable volume, but that volume is built from painted value blocks and
deliberately designed shadow shapes — never from a lighting simulation. Skin is painted colour
in clear tonal steps with brush-defined edges: no subsurface glow, no specular highlight on the
nose, lips or forehead, no rendered sheen anywhere on skin.

Hair is painted as shaped masses and curl clusters with a strong graphic silhouette and a few
drawn accent strands — never individually rendered strands, never volumetric hair. Brows,
freckles, fabric pattern, wall plaster and wood grain are painted marks sitting on the surface
as brushwork.

Shadows are designed shapes with readable edges, not soft raytraced falloff. Light is warm and
motivated, but painted in rather than simulated. Depth comes from staging, overlap, scale and
atmospheric value — never from rendered depth of field; backgrounds may soften, but as painted
softness, never as lens bokeh.

The painting exists ON the objects. Do not lay a paper grain, watercolour wash or canvas filter
over the image.

There is no uniform cartoon outline. Silhouettes read through value separation and painted
edges, with selective dark graphic creases where naturally motivated.

Skin, curls, plaid cotton, canvas apron, cream paper panels, graphite, ink and restrained
test colour each keep their own distinct painted character.

CRITICAL DISTINCTION: the room is painted with depth and the drawings are not. Every fox, cat and dragon
study exists only as flat ink and graphite bonded to the page — no thickness, no
volume, no lift, no shadow of its own — and none of them ever animates, detaches or becomes a
creature.

Maintain stable facial construction, eye size, curl mass, body proportions, wardrobe and
material character across every lens and angle.

Animation is authored pose to pose: strong silhouettes, clear anticipation, physical weight,
clean arcs and expressive facial acting — intentionally animated, never motion-captured or
mechanically interpolated.

ABSOLUTELY NOT 3D: no CGI, no Pixar or DreamWorks render look, no Unreal or Blender render, no
PBR materials, no subsurface scattering, no specular skin, no rendered hair strands, no
depth-of-field bokeh, no photorealism, no waxy or plastic skin, no AI-smoothed faces. Also not
flat two-tone cel anime, no uniform black outlines, no watercolour or canvas overlay, no muddy
colour, no airbrushed softness, no excessive bloom and no unmotivated neon rim light.

################################################################
PHYSICAL BEHAVIOUR CONTRACT
################################################################

Everything in frame has mass. Objects resist, drag, settle and take time to stop; nothing
slides weightlessly or snaps to a pose. Contact is literal: fingers close in sequence and grip
with real pressure, feet transfer weight, and anything resting on a surface stays in contact
with it and carries its own painted contact shadow.

Motion carries overlap and follow-through. What is pulled trails a beat behind the hand that
pulls it; cloth, curls, cable and loose contents lag and resettle late rather than moving
locked to the body.

Materials behave as themselves — each with its own stiffness, weight and sound.

Depth is real and readable: distance across a space reads as distance, and the camera never
cheats an object closer than it is.

Repeated objects are individuated, never cloned: each keeps its own creases, orientation and
wear.

Emotion is held rather than performed: tension stays in the jaw, shoulders and tempo, and does
not release between actions.

################################################################
OUTGOING CONTINUITY
################################################################

HIXXA has cleared the three rings and continues feet-first through the open torn-paper chasm. The huge dragon-shaped cast shadow lies across the far wall. Her red pencil remains in her curls. Exactly thirty crumpled-paper balls descend around her. The rings remain above.
